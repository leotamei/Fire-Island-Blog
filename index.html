Adventures in Fire Island

My Trip Experience & Highlights

My recent trip to Fire Island National Seashore was an unforgettable coastal getaway. Located off the southern shore of Long Island, New York, this tranquil barrier island offers pristine ocean beaches, historic architecture, and car-free neighborhoods accessible only by ferry.

During my stay, I hiked through the ancient maritime Sunken Forest, climbed 182 steps up the historic Fire Island Lighthouse for panoramic views of the Great South Bay, and observed wild white-tailed deer roaming peacefully among the beach grass.
